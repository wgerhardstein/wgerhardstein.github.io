Multi-Strategy Trading Research Platform

39M1-min bars

A research program for deciding when a backtest can be trusted. I tested futures strategy configurations across 39 million one-minute bars and rejected 7,507 as indistinguishable from noise. The project documents the decisions behind the result, including catching a deadline calculation that was 50% too generous and declining to deploy a top-scoring strategy book that failed on unseen data.

  • Walk-forward validation
  • 340K simulated paths

An automated research pipeline for stocks and options. A nightly scanner pulls earnings calendars and SEC filings, runs a panel of AI analyst agents on each candidate, applies guardrails and risk-based position sizing, and delivers a morning briefing to Telegram and Notion. A daily monitor tracks paper-trading positions and flags thesis changes, stop-losses, and profit targets.
